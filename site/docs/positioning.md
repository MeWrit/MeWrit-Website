# MeWriT: identity and pitch

Draft for Dr Hetal Shah, 8 October 2026. Everything here is drawn from the MeWriT deck (July 2026) and Dr Shah's CV (August 2026).

## What MeWriT is
A specialist medical writing and scientific communication consultancy for the clinical research and healthcare sector, based in Ahmedabad and working with sponsors, CROs, device makers, institutions and clinician-researchers in India and abroad. Five practices, one standard: documents that regulators, journals and clinicians can act on.

## The five practices
1. Regulatory writing: clinical trial and regulatory documentation (protocols for every design, informed consent, investigator's brochures, clinical study reports to ICH E3, CTD modules 2.4 to 2.7, marketing authorisation dossiers, patient and event narratives, CIPs and CERs, responses to regulatory queries).
2. Scientific publications: original research manuscripts, literature reviews, case reports, medical data analysis, abstracts, posters and congress presentations, to ICMJE and GPP.
3. Medical communications: position papers, consensus documents, real-world evidence from protocol to report to publication, expert statements, writing support to medical professionals, educational materials.
4. Training (MeWriT Academy): structured, hands-on workshops, basic to advanced, half a day to three days, tailored to the audience; open workshops and courses through the Academy.
5. AI/ML advisory: subject-matter expertise for AI/ML solution teams, human-in-the-loop review of generated content for accuracy, relevance and quality, usability testing, expert webinars.

## The pitch, in thirty seconds
Clinical research only counts when it is written to the standard the decision needs. MeWriT writes that standard: submission-ready regulatory documents for sponsors and CROs, publication-ready manuscripts and congress materials for clinicians and researchers, and strategic communications for medical affairs. We train the people who write them, through the MeWriT Academy, and we bring the same domain judgement to teams building AI for medical writing. Twenty-two years in clinical research, 100+ trial documents, 30+ ICH E3 clinical study reports, 70+ publications and presentations, 3,000+ professionals trained. Writing Science Right.

## Positioning decisions
- Services first, not founder-led. Credibility comes from the figures and the published acknowledgements; Dr Shah appears once, in a compact "who leads the work" band, with a link to her profile.
- Five practices a client chooses from, often more than one. Never presented as steps or a process.
- Audiences: pharma and biotech sponsors, CROs, device and nutraceutical makers, hospitals and academic institutions, professional societies, clinician-researchers, AI/ML solution teams.
- Tone: plain, exact, confident; British spelling; no superlatives.
- Not said: client names without permission (none are used); invented figures (the two illustrative scenes say so on the page); "founder-led".
